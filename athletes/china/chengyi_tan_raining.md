谭成义——训练体系

1.训练体系概述

当前公开的训练框架

谭成义与王凯胜共同公开提出了推/拉/腿训练框架。

该体系强调：

-动作质量
-渐进超负荷
-根据生物力学选择训练动作
-疲劳管理
-充分的恢复
-高质量的工作组
-避免大重量复合动作做到力竭
-让小肌群孤立动作接近力竭

重要提示：

本文档介绍了谭成义公开演示的训练实践。

这并不意味着每个组成部分在科学上都优于所有其他训练体系。

---

2.拆分结构

推

主要肌群：

-胸部
-前束/侧束三角肌
-肱三头肌

已记录的练习：

1.杠铃卧推
2.上斜哑铃卧推
3.双杠臂屈伸
4.仰卧臂屈伸
5.Y字飞鸟/侧平举

训练逻辑

推举训练包含：

-一项主要的推举动作
-一项额外的胸推
-一项自重/复合推举动作
- direct triceps work
- direct lateral-delt work

这会产生一种组合：

- high mechanical loading
- muscle-specific work
- relatively efficient exercise selection

---

3. Pull

Primary muscles:

- latissimus dorsi
- upper back
- rear delts
- biceps

Reported exercises:

1. Single-arm cable pulldown
2. Neutral-grip pulldown
3. Single-arm supported row
4. Elbow-out / upper-back row
5. Cable curl

Training logic

The structure combines:

- vertical pulling
- unilateral pulling
- horizontal pulling
- upper-back work
- direct biceps work

The use of unilateral pulling allows individual sides to be trained separately.

This may be useful when an athlete has:

- asymmetry
- different strength between sides
- different scapular mechanics
- difficulty finding a symmetrical bilateral pulling pattern

However, unilateral work should not automatically be interpreted as superior to bilateral training.

---

4. Legs

Primary muscles:

- quadriceps
- hamstrings
- glutes
- spinal erectors
- hip stabilizers

Reported exercises:

1. Single-leg dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2. Bulgarian split squat
3. Squat / front squat variation
4. Barbell Romanian deadlift
5. Back extension

Training logic

The session combines:

- unilateral lower-body training
- squat pattern
- hip hinge
- posterior-chain work
- spinal-erector work

This creates a relatively posterior-chain-heavy lower-body structure.

---

5. Core Training Philosophy

Principle 1 — Quality before load

A heavier weight is not automatically better.

Progression should preserve:

- stable technique
- target-muscle loading
- appropriate range of motion
- controlled execution

If increasing load causes a major deterioration in technique, the increase may not represent useful progression.

---

6. Principle 2 — Compound movements and isolation movements should not be treated identically

Public explanations associated with this system emphasize different fatigue-management strategies for large compound lifts and smaller isolation exercises.

Compound movements

Examples:

- bench press
- squat
- Romanian deadlift

General approach:

- maintain technical margin
- avoid unnecessary failure
- use controlled RPE/RIR
- allow adequate recovery

Isolation movements

Examples:

- lateral raise
- curl
- cable work

These movements can generally be trained closer to failure because:

- systemic fatigue is usually lower
- technique breakdown is easier to control
- recovery cost is generally smaller

Important:

"Never train compounds to failure" should NOT be treated as an absolute scientific law.

The better Skill rule is:

«The closer an exercise is to technical failure, the more its fatigue cost and safety/technique consequences must be considered.»

---

7. Progressive Overload

The system places substantial emphasis on progression rather than randomly changing exercises.

Possible progression variables:

1. load
2. repetitions
3. execution quality
4. range of motion
5. proximity to failure
6. total productive volume

A practical progression hierarchy:

Stage 1

Improve technique at the same load.

Stage 2

Increase repetitions while maintaining technique.

Stage 3

Increase load while maintaining the target repetition range.

Stage 4

Continue progression until recovery or performance indicates that volume/intensity needs adjustment.

---

8. RIR / RPE Framework

A useful interpretation of the training philosophy:

Heavy compound movements

Typical target:

- approximately 1–3 RIR
- approximately RPE 7–9

The exact target should depend on:

- exercise
- training phase
- fatigue
- experience
- recovery

Isolation movements

Typical target:

- approximately 0–2 RIR
- sometimes technical failure

Again, this is a practical framework, not a rigid rule.

---

9. Rest and Recovery

The public training material emphasizes that PPL does NOT necessarily mean:

“3天训练+1天休息”

训练周期可根据恢复情况灵活调整。

可能的训练安排：

高恢复

推→拉→腿→休息

中等恢复

推→拉→休息→腿→推→休息……

低恢复

推 → 休息 → 拉 → 休息 → 腿部 → 休息

关键变量并非训练分割的名称。

重要的变量有：

- weekly productive volume
- training quality
-表现趋势
- muscle recovery
- joint recovery
- sleep
- life stress

---

10. Natural-Athlete Interpretation

对于自然健美而言，最有用的一课并不是：

«“每个人都应该像谭成义一样训练。”»

而是：

“构建一套既能提供充分刺激，又能让运动员反复进行高质量训练的训练体系。”

这一点对于天赋型运动员尤为重要，因为没有任何理由认为，精英运动员的训练量或训练频率就一定能够被每一位训练者所完全恢复。

-精确的组数
-精确的重复次数
-精确的每周训练频率
-精确的休息安排
-精确的RPE
-精确的训练进度
-精确的训练量

-遗传因素
- 训练年限

# 13. Competition Preparation

Chengyi Tan's competition preparation can be used as a case study for:

- Maintaining resistance training during fat loss
- Managing training fatigue
- Preserving muscle mass
- Adjusting volume according to recovery
- Integrating diet and training
- Monitoring performance during a deficit

Important principle:

> During a cutting phase, the objective is not to maximize training volume. The objective is to preserve or maximize useful training stimulus while managing fatigue.

---

# 14. Training Quality Control

For every exercise, evaluate:

### Technique

- Stable setup
- Consistent execution
- Appropriate ROM
- Controlled eccentric
- Appropriate force direction

### Stimulus

- Target muscle receives sufficient tension
- Load is appropriate
- Repetitions remain productive

### Fatigue

- Does the exercise create excessive systemic fatigue?
- Does it impair later exercises?
- Does it impair recovery between sessions?

The preferred exercise is not necessarily the exercise with the greatest absolute load.

# 15. Natural Athlete Case Interpretation

Chengyi Tan should NOT be interpreted as:

> "This is the correct natural bodybuilding program."

Instead:

> "This is one high-level natural bodybuilding case from which useful training principles can be extracted."

The Skill must compare his training practices with:

- Scientific evidence
- Other natural athletes
- The user's individual response

# 17. Data Reliability

Every athlete-specific training variable must be classified.

### Documented

Directly visible or verified from the athlete's own material.

### Athlete-Reported

Explicitly stated by the athlete.

### Secondary Source

Reported by another credible source.

### Derived

Calculated or inferred from available evidence.

### Unknown

Insufficient evidence.

Never convert:

> UNKNOWN → Assumed Fact

---

# 18. Variables That Must Not Be Invented

Unless directly verified, do NOT invent:

- Exact weekly sets
- Exact weekly frequency
- Exact RIR
- Exact rest intervals
- Exact progression scheme
- Exact deload schedule
- Exact exercise rotation
- Exact yearly training volume
- Exact calorie intake
- Exact macronutrient intake

These variables should remain:

>未知

until sufficient evidence becomes available.

#20. 核心要点

从陈毅谭案中可以总结出的主要可迁移经验教训是：

1.训练质量比盲目增加训练量更为重要。
2.训练频率是实现高效训练的手段之一。
3. 高频率训练需要充分的恢复。
4. 运动项目的选择应因人而异。
5. 进阶是多维度的。
6. 每次训练后的疲劳程度应得到合理控制。
7. 训练方案应根据运动员当前的状态进行调整。
8.营养与训练应统筹评估。
9.高水平运动员的实践仅属个案研究，并非普遍适用的证据。
10. 最终的训练建议应由科学证据、运动员个案数据及个体反应共同决定。
