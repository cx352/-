## 5. Training Philosophy

Secondary public material associated with Chengyi Tan describes several recurring principles:

### Core principles
1. Training quality over simply increasing load.
2. Emphasis on movement execution and target-muscle sensation.
3. Progressive overload should be controlled rather than blindly adding weight.
4. Exercise selection should consider individual biomechanics and joint position.
5. Training frequency should be adjusted according to recovery.
6. Heavy compound movements should retain repetitions in reserve.
7. Isolation movements can be taken much closer to failure.

### Important caution
These are athlete/practitioner methods, not automatically scientifically proven rules.

The Skill must distinguish:

- "Tan's practice"
from
- "Evidence-supported hypertrophy principle."

## 9. What We Can Learn From This Athlete

### High-value observations
Potentially useful principles to investigate further:

1. Long-term consistency is more important than short-term extreme dieting.
2. Training quality is repeatedly emphasized.
3. Progression should be controlled by recovery and performance.
4. Natural athletes need to manage fatigue because recovery capacity limits training frequency and volume.
5. Diet should support training rather than simply minimize bodyweight.
6. Contest preparation should be treated as a specialized phase, not a normal lifestyle.
