# Training State Decision Engine

## 1. Purpose

This module determines the athlete's current training state and selects the appropriate training strategy.

It works together with:

- state_assessment.md
- phase_selector.md
- bulking.md
- chengyi_tan_training.md

The system should not treat training and nutrition as independent variables.

The final recommendation should integrate:

> PHYSICAL STATE → NUTRITION PHASE → TRAINING STATE → TRAINING PLAN

---

# 2. Training State Inputs

Evaluate the following information whenever available.

## Bodyweight

- Current bodyweight
- 7-day average
- 14-day average
- 28-day trend

## Body Composition

- Waist circumference
- Estimated body-fat percentage
- Progress photos
- Recent competition condition

## Nutrition

- Current calories
- Protein
- Carbohydrates
- Fat
- Current calorie deficit/surplus
- Recent dietary changes

## Training Performance

- Main exercise performance
- Repetition performance
- Load progression
- Pump and muscular performance
- Training quality
- Performance trend over 7–28 days

## Recovery

- Sleep duration
- Sleep quality
- General fatigue
- Muscle soreness
- Joint discomfort
- Motivation
- Training readiness

## Activity

- Daily steps
- Cardio
- Recent activity changes

# 3. Training State Classification

Classify the athlete into one primary training state.

## A. Productive Training

Characteristics:

- Performance stable or improving
- Recovery acceptable
- Sleep acceptable
- Motivation acceptable
- Muscle soreness manageable
- No major joint problems
- Training quality high

Strategy:

> Maintain normal training structure and progression.

Do not reduce training volume simply because the athlete is not gaining bodyweight rapidly.

---

## B. Energy-Limited Training

Characteristics:

- Bodyweight declining
- Calorie deficit present
- Training performance beginning to decline
- Recovery worsening
- Hunger increasing
- Sleep may be affected

Strategy:

> Preserve high-quality training stimulus while reducing unnecessary fatigue.

Priority:

1. Maintain exercise quality
2. Maintain reasonable training intensity
3. Reduce excessive volume if necessary
4. Coordinate with nutrition adjustment

Do not automatically remove all hard training.

---

## C. Recovery-Limited Training

Characteristics:

- Persistent fatigue
- Performance declining
- Poor sleep
- High soreness
- Reduced motivation
- Reduced training quality
- Recovery does not improve despite adequate nutrition

Strategy:

> Reduce training stress temporarily.

Possible adjustments:

- Reduce weekly sets
- Increase recovery days
- Increase RIR
- Reduce failure training
- Consider a deload

---

## D. Productive Gaining Training

Characteristics:

- Bodyweight slowly increasing
- Waist reasonably controlled
- Performance improving
- Recovery good
- Nutrition supports training

Strategy:

> Gradually progress training volume and/or performance when required.

Do not automatically increase volume every week.

Progression should occur only when the current workload is being recovered from successfully.

---

## E. Recomposition Training

Characteristics:

- Bodyweight stable or slowly decreasing
- Waist stable or decreasing
- Performance stable or improving
- Recovery acceptable
- Athlete has sufficient training experience

Strategy:

> Maintain a high-quality hypertrophy stimulus while controlling unnecessary fatigue.

Avoid aggressive reductions in training volume.

---

## F. Plateau Training

Characteristics:

- Performance unchanged for multiple weeks
- Body composition unchanged
- Recovery appears adequate
- Nutrition adherence is adequate

Before changing training, investigate:

1. Exercise execution
2. Progressive overload
3. Weekly training volume
4. Exercise selection
5. Training frequency
6. Recovery
7. Nutrition
8. Activity

Do not automatically add more sets.

---

# 4. Training Phase and Training State Are Different

The system must distinguish between:

### Nutrition Phase

Examples:

- Post-contest recovery
- Fat loss
- Recomposition
- Conservative gain
- Standard gain
- Maintenance

and:

### Training State

Examples:

- Productive
- Energy-limited
- Recovery-limited
- Productive gaining
- Recomposition
- Plateau

These two classifications must interact.

Example:

> Nutrition phase = calorie deficit  
> Training state = productive

does NOT automatically mean low-volume training.

Example:

> Nutrition phase = calorie deficit  
> Training state = recovery-limited

may require a temporary reduction in training volume.

# 5. Training Prescription Framework

After determining training state, generate the training plan.

The plan should include:

- Training split
- Training frequency
- Muscle-group frequency
- Weekly sets per muscle group
- Exercise selection
- Sets per exercise
- Repetitions
- RIR/RPE
- Rest intervals
- Exercise order
- Progression method
- Deload requirements

---

# 6. Chengyi Tan Case Template

When generating the actual training plan, use Chengyi Tan's documented public training practices as the primary athlete case template.

The following information should be treated as:

> Athlete-specific empirical practice.

It should NOT automatically be treated as universal scientific law.

The system should distinguish between:

### Scientific evidence

Used to determine general training principles.

and:

### Chengyi Tan case data

Used to construct the practical training template.

---

# 8. Training Volume Adjustment

Training volume should change according to the athlete's response.

## If performance improves and recovery is good

> Maintain volume or increase gradually if necessary.

Do not automatically increase volume.

---

## If performance is stable and recovery is good

> Maintain current volume.

There is no requirement to increase volume every week.

---

## If performance declines slightly

First investigate:

- sleep
- nutrition
- activity
- exercise execution
- accumulated fatigue

Do not immediately reduce volume.

---

## If performance declines consistently

If the decline persists across multiple sessions and recovery is poor:

> Reduce training stress.

Possible actions:

- reduce sets
- increase RIR
- reduce failure work
- increase rest
- add recovery days
